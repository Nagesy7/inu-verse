# Good Boy Academy (Phase 1)

Walkable classroom north of Market Row (next to the Pack Clubhouse). Dean Barkley staffs the desk.

## Board UI
Lesson of the Day · Quiz · Practice Launch (stub) · Pup Report Card.

- **Bones:** first clear of a lesson pays ~15–40 via `earnBones` (outfit bonus applies). Daily quiz full clear pays a small Bones bump.
- **Quiz:** 5 cards/day from an 8-card bank (Sydney day). **3-strike day cap.** Streak pin flavour at 3 consecutive clear days.
- **Disclaimer** always on the board: educational game fiction, not investment advice.
- **Report Card:** owned `breedPups` (max 6) as classmates — flavour only.
- **No** $KIBBLE / Coach fee. **Pawmarks untouched.** Practice Launch sandbox is Phase 1b (no `/api/launch` chain tx).
